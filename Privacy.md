# Privacy Policy — DocLens

**Last updated:** 6 October 2026

DocLens ("the app", "we", "us") is a document scanning and management app
developed for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles
and how your privacy is protected.

**In short: DocLens is built to work on your device.** Your scanned documents,
their contents, and your AI chats stay on your phone. We do not have user
accounts, we do not upload your documents to any server, and we do not sell or
share your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on our
servers.** DocLens has no user accounts and no backend that receives your data.

All content you create or import — scanned documents, PDFs, images, extracted
text, QR codes, and AI conversations — is stored **locally on your device**.

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Everything the app does is processed on your device:

- **Document scanning** uses your device camera to capture pages. Scans are
  saved only in the app's local storage.
- **Text recognition (OCR)** runs entirely on-device using Google ML Kit. Image
  and text data are not sent anywhere.
- **PDF tools** (merge, split, compress, protect, watermark, e-sign, extract
  text) process your files locally on your device.
- **QR code** generation and scanning are performed on-device.
- **AI Document Assistant / Ask AI** runs a language model **locally on your
  device**. Your documents and chat messages are processed on-device and are not
  sent to any external service.
- **Search** indexes only document names and runs locally.

## 3. Internet Access

DocLens requests internet permission for a single purpose:

- **One-time AI model download.** The first time you use an AI feature, the app
  downloads the on-device language model (Qwen2.5-0.5B) from a public model host
  (Hugging Face). This download fetches model files only; it does **not** send
  any of your documents, text, or personal information. Once downloaded, the AI
  runs fully offline.

No other feature requires an internet connection, and no usage data, analytics,
or crash reports are transmitted.

## 4. Permissions We Use

- **Camera** — to scan documents and read QR codes. Images are used only within
  the app and stored locally.
- **Internet** — only for the one-time AI model download described above.
- **Biometric / Device credential** — if you enable App Lock, your fingerprint,
  face, or PIN is verified by Android's secure system. DocLens never sees or
  stores your biometric data.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All your documents and data are stored in the app's private storage on your
  device.
- Optional **App Lock** (biometric or PIN) lets you restrict access to the app.
- You can protect individual PDFs with a password using the built-in tools.
- Because your data lives on your device, keeping your device secure (screen
  lock, up-to-date OS) is the best way to protect it.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** share, sell, or rent your data to anyone. Content leaves your
device only when **you** choose to share or export it (for example, using the
Android share sheet, printing, or saving a file to another location). In those
cases, the content goes only where you direct it.

## 7. Third-Party Services

DocLens uses the following on-device libraries/components. They operate locally
and do not receive your personal content:

- **Google ML Kit** — on-device text recognition and QR scanning.
- **Google Play Services (code scanner)** — on-device QR scanning.
- **On-device AI model** — downloaded once from a public model host, then run
  locally.

## 8. Children's Privacy

DocLens is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any personal
information from anyone, including children.

## 9. Your Choices

- You can delete any document within the app at any time.
- You can clear all app data from your device's system Settings → Apps →
  DocLens → Storage, which removes all documents and the downloaded AI model.
- You can uninstall the app at any time to remove all associated data.
